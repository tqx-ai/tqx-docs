1. Describe or select what you want to explore

Tell the agent:

- Which market you are interested in.
- Which factors to study.
- What strategy you want to test.
- Your preferred time period and holding horizon.
- Any backtest.

Or select a card:

Browse the available starting points. Click the card that best matches your goal. 

- **HK Strategies** — Hong Kong trend and breakout strategies.
- **US Strategies** — US stock strategy templates.
- **Factor Research** — Momentum, volume, and correlation analysis.
- **Common Tools** — Backtesting, optimization, and strategy summaries. 

QUBE will use the selected card as the starting point for your conversation.

2. Optimize the strategy and do the backtest

Let QUBE run the research or backtest, then review the results in the panel on the right. Check the **total return, annualized return, maximum drawdown, Sharpe ratio, number of trades, and final equity**. Use the chat box to adjust the strategy, rerun the test, and compare the updated results.<br />
