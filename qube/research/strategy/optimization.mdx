Strategy tuning varies numeric keys already declared and referenced in `STRATEGY_PARAMS`. It freezes the source, backtest configuration, costs, capital, and factor binding. Dates and capital are not strategy-code search dimensions.

## Design a bounded comparison

Use a small candidate set, typically 16 total including the baseline, with at most 32 after baseline insertion and deduplication. Supply sorted unique actual `tradingDates` (2–4000 ISO dates) from the market calendar; do not guess weekdays.

Validation is enabled by default, splitting trading dates into 70% research and 30% validation. Choose `total_return` or `sharpe` to maximize, or `max_drawdown` to minimize absolute drawdown. Compare both research and validation evidence rather than selecting on the research score alone.

## Apply and recheck

Review candidate failures and assumptions. Applying a selected candidate requires expected source and configuration revisions and updates the current strategy configuration. It does not create a named version or make a trading decision.

Run an ordinary backtest with the applied candidate, inspect results, then create a version if appropriate. Recheck another period and realistic cost assumptions. See [tuning commands and JSON](/qube/api/runs).
