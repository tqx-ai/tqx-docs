A Qube schedule starts a new Qube conversation at each trigger. Use a one-time date, an interval, or a cron expression with an explicit timezone. Review the prompt and execution history after each run.

Schedules create conversations; they do not silently publish a Factor, Strategy, Version, or trading order. Any generated research result still needs validation, review, and an explicit save or run action. Stop or edit a schedule when its prompt or cadence is no longer appropriate.
