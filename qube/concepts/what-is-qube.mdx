Qube is an AI workspace for quantitative research. It helps you turn a market question into code, run that code against controlled data, and keep the evidence attached to the research asset.

## What Qube connects

- **Conversation** gives you a place to describe a hypothesis and revise the work.
- **Workspace** gives you files, folders, uploaded material, knowledge, and task outputs.
- **Data Skills** explain the fields and methods available in the Qube runtime.
- **Factors and strategies** are durable research assets with source and configuration.
- **Runs** freeze the inputs used for an analysis or backtest.
- **Results** expose metrics, charts, trades, logs, and diagnostics.
- **Versions** preserve named snapshots that you can compare, bind, or restore.

## What Qube does not decide for you

Qube can generate and evaluate research code, but it does not turn a historical result into a trading recommendation. You decide which hypothesis to test, how to interpret the evidence, and whether any later action is authorized.

## Qube and trading

Research and trading are connected through review. Qube can help you produce a strategy and test it historically. Account access, order submission, and live execution remain in the [Trading](/trading/info) module.
