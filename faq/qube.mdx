## What is Qube? {#what-is-qube}

Qube is TQX's AI workspace for quantitative research. It connects questions, data, code, runs, results, and named versions. Start with the [overview](/qube) or [quickstart](/qube/research/info).

## How can I use it? {#access}

Use web conversations and research libraries, [Qube CLI](/qube/cli/quickstart), or the [TypeScript SDK](/qube/sdk/overview). They operate on the same research assets.

## Does saving run the code? {#history}

No. Saving source, saving configuration, executing a run, and creating a named version are separate actions. A named version comes from a successful ordinary run. See [research objects](/qube/concepts/research-objects) and [run history](/qube/research/run-history).

## Can a generated strategy trade live? {#live-trading}

Yes. A coding agent can use `tqx research` to read and evaluate a Qube strategy and its backtest results, then use `tqx trading` to query a connected account and execute trades within its authorized scope. Configure the agent to assess signal timing, liquidity, costs, risk, and evidence on other periods. A backtest simulates trades; live execution uses the [Trading workflow](/trading/info). Historical results cannot guarantee future performance.

## Which data API should I use? {#data}

Choose the [Qube runtime](/data/qube-runtime) or [local API-key package](/data/local-package). Qube's native Skill provides historical data, not live Tick quotes. Strategy engine symbols and data-package symbols can differ.

## Where are my files and knowledge? {#research-and-execution}

See [workspace files](/qube/workspace), [conversations](/qube/concepts/conversations), and [personal knowledge](/qube/workspace/knowledge). Files and conversation text do not automatically become formal factors or strategies.

## Where do I check plans and usage? {#subscriptions-and-usage}

Use the [subscription page](https://www.tqx.ai/en/payment) and the usage information in your signed-in Qube account. Prices, eligibility, and allowances are shown there. Model and compute use vary by task; a failed or cancelled task can still consume resources.

### Plans {#plans}

Check current terms on the subscription page before subscribing.

### Billing {#billing}

Review billing records and remaining usage in your account. There is no fixed combined cost for generating a strategy and running a backtest.
