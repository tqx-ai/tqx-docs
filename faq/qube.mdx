## Research and execution {#research-and-execution}

### Can a generated strategy be used for live trading? {#live-trading}

<Warning>
A successful backtest does not establish how a strategy will perform in live trading. Signal timing, execution prices, liquidity, fees, and capital requirements can affect results.
</Warning>

Before connecting a live account, you can test the strategy in a [simulation account](https://www.tqx.ai/en/user-center?tab=agents) and compare its signals and execution with the backtest. Qube-generated content is for research and does not constitute investment advice.

### What data does Qube use? {#data}

Qube provides Hong Kong and US market data, reference data, financial statements, and market factors. Research symbols require a market suffix:

- Hong Kong: `.HK`, for example `0001.HK` or `0700.HK`.
- US NASDAQ BASIC: `.NB`, for example `AAPL.NB`.

These are research data symbols. Trading commands use the symbol format specified in the [trading API documentation](/trading/api/place-order). Daily price data is not adjusted for corporate actions. See the [data guide](/data/info) for coverage and limitations.

### How can I use Qube? {#access}

You can use Qube research tools through [TQX CLI](/cli) with a coding agent. The Qube web interface also supports research and backtesting. Available features may change as the product is updated.

## Subscriptions and usage {#subscriptions-and-usage}

### Where can I view subscription plans? {#plans}

The available plans are Free, Basic, and Pro. New users can claim a seven-day Basic trial, subject to the eligibility shown on the [subscription page](https://www.tqx.ai/en/payment).

| Plan | Monthly price | Annual price | Monthly credits | Simulation accounts (\$10M each) | Live accounts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | \$0 | \$0 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| Basic | \$19 | \$182 | 1,500 | 3 | Unlimited |
| Pro | \$49 | \$470 | 4,500 | 10 | Unlimited |

<Note>
Free and Basic include basic community access. Pro includes premium community access and a dedicated consultant with 24/7 support. Confirm current prices, benefits, and trial eligibility on the subscription page before subscribing.
</Note>

### Where can I check usage and billing? {#billing}

Sign in and open the [subscription page](https://www.tqx.ai/en/payment). Use **Bill Records** to review billing records and **My Subscription** to check your plan. Credit usage varies by task; strategy creation and backtesting do not have a single fixed combined cost.
