## Choose your starting point

- **Research**: Start with [Qube](/qube) to explore data, research factors and strategies, and review analyses or backtests.
- **Data**: Choose the [Qube runtime](/data/qube-runtime) or [local package](/data/local-package), then find your [data API](/data/info).
- **Trading**: Start with the [trading guide](/trading/info). Follow the [CLI guide](/cli) to install `tqx` and authenticate.

## What each module covers

- **Guides** explain product-wide setup, CLI access, trading, FAQs, and support.
- **Qube** explains the research workspace and its web, CLI, and SDK interfaces.
- **Data** documents the `tqx_data` methods and fields available in each environment.
