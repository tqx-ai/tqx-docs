## What Qube is for

Qube helps you connect a market hypothesis to evidence. It keeps research source, parameters, runs, results, and versions tied together so you can compare experiments and review assumptions before acting.

Qube supports research in Hong Kong and US markets. Historical research is evidence about the selected code, data, parameters, and period. Review results and authorize any trading action separately.
