TQX provides Hong Kong and US historical market, reference, financial, and factor data for research. Choose the environment before copying an API example.

## Symbols and availability

Hong Kong data uses symbols such as `0700.HK`. Qube native `tqx_data` queries for US historical prices, market reference, financial statements, and factors use bare symbols such as `AAPL`. Returned `symbol` and `stock_symbol` columns also use bare symbols. When selecting the market, use `market="us"` or `type="us"` for factor queries.

The US strategy engine uses symbols such as `AAPL.NB`. Follow [Write a strategy](/qube/research/strategy/code) for the format used in strategy code.

The Qube data examples here query historical snapshots. For live subscriptions, see [Local Tick streaming](/data/market-data/tick). Verify coverage, missing fields, timezones, adjustment behavior, and announcement dates. Historical financial values must have been available at the decision time.
