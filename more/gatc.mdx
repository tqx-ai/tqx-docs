# entry部分的问题

1. What are the entry requirements?<br />All of the following are required:
   - The participant is at least 18 and legally permitted to trade securities
   - A competition account has been registered on TQX
   - A supported broker has been linked. tiger, Panda Securities and Zhangle are currently supported
   - 根据香港的规定，开证券账户需要10000hkd的入金，但是对于gatc大赛来说，我们只需要高于1000hkd，即可进入轻量组。
   - A verifiable, original AI strategy is submitted through TQX-CLI and passes review

   Phase 1 covers Hong Kong stocks and U.S. stocks. Once the review is approved, the account enters that month’s leaderboard and the points system.
2. When can I register?  How are the active days counted?
3. Is GATC live trading or paper trading?<br />Live trading. Participants bind a live brokerage account at a partner broker. Results are produced from that account, which distinguishes GATC from paper-trading contests.
4. Is a Qube membership required before entry?<br />Entry requires a competition account, a live brokerage account, and an original strategy submitted through TQX-CLI. A Qube membership and compute credits are for research and backtesting. They are not a condition for being scored on the leaderboard.
5. May a strategy built in Qube be submitted directly?<br />Competition materials are submitted only through TQX-CLI. A participant may revise a strategy built in Qube and then submit it through the CLI. A public template submitted without revision is not treated as an original strategy.
6. How is an entry completed?<br />- Obtain an API key. Join the competition, create the trading account, and select the account type. **GATC accepts only a live account**. Submit the entry in My Agents, select the local model, and obtain the API key.<br />- Configure the CLI. Enter the API key in the AI agent you use. Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, Trae, GitHub Copilot, and Windsurf are supported.<br />- After configuration, use the prompts on the following page to build a strategy, refine it, and issue order instructions: [https://c.tqx.ai/e/workshop](https://c.tqx.ai/e/workshop)
7. How are daily position checks and weekly rebalances scheduled?<br />The competition system does not execute a live strategy on a daily or weekly schedule. The participant's AI agent must schedule the position check and rebalance. A daily task checks positions and rebalances daily. The weekly rebalance requires a separate weekly task. <br />- **Cursor**: use Automations in the Agent Window.<br />- **Codex**: use Scheduled to create a daily or weekly task.<br />- **Claude Code**: use Routines. Run /schedule in the CLI, or create one at [claude.ai/code/routines](http://claude.ai/code/routines). The frequency may be daily or weekly. A cloud routine continues to run after the computer is closed. A local scheduled task in the Desktop app runs only while the application is open and the computer is awake.

5. How is a quarter judged with two Lightweight months and one Heavyweight month? How is the year judged?<br />For a quarter or a year, the group is the one that appears more often among valid participation months. If the counts are equal, the period is assigned to the Heavyweight Group. Points are the sum of the monthly totals. The pool is not recalculated.

   A month with a composite score of 0, without valid competition capital, or under a mandatory cooling-off period is not a valid participation month. A quarterly award requires at least 2 valid participation months. An annual award requires at least 8.

# awards部分的问题

1. **How are awards paid, and when do they arrive?**<br />Staff will contact the recipient by email. Please check the message and verify that the sender’s email suffix is an official address, so that the message is not a fraudulent request. Staff will collect a bank account number and other payment details.

   The award is paid within five business days after the period ends. A public holiday may delay receipt. Transfer fees are borne by each party. The recipient is responsible for understanding the applicable local tax rules and for meeting the tax obligation on the award.
2. What does each rank receive?<br />The Lightweight Group and the Heavyweight Group are ranked separately. Amounts are in HKD and are paid per person. For a range such as 4th–10th, the figure is the award for each recipient in that range. Vacant ranks are not rolled up.
